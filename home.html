The Sacred Protocol

The 3-Step Shiro Abhyanga Ritual

An ancient mindful evening practice connecting root, crown, and spirit.

I

Awaken & Warm

Dispense 5–8 drops of elixir into your palms or warm the flacon in a bowl of warm water for 2 minutes to activate the botanical terpenes. Cup your hands and inhale the grounding aroma of Himalayan woods.

II

Marma Point Scalp Massage

Part your hair and press the oil directly into the scalp. Using firm circular motions with your fingertips or the Kansa wand, stimulate the *Adhipati* (crown) and *Simanta* Marma points to boost blood circulation.

III

Rest & Rinse

Leave the herbal oil on for at least 45 minutes or overnight wrapped in silk. Rinse with lukewarm water and a sulfate-free cleanser. Witness hair transformed with mirror-like weightless gloss.
